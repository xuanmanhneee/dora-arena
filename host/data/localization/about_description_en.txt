Dora Arena is a 2D multiplayer fighting game inspired by the world of Doraemon.
Players control Mini-Dora characters and engage in fast-paced battles inside energy arenas that require quick reflexes and precise timing.

Each match takes place in a limited space where players must:
- Move skillfully to dodge attacks
- Time their attacks carefully
- Use special abilities effectively
- Push opponents out of the safe zone

Current game modes:
- 1v1 (Local)
- 2v2 (LAN)

Key features:
- Local multiplayer
- Mobile controller support
- Fast-paced arena combat
- Lightweight networking architecture

Technology used:
- Godot 4.6.1 Stable
- GDScript

Development status:
- Alpha Build
- Work in Progress
